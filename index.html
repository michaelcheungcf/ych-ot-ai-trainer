⚠ General hip precaution reference

  • Avoid bending the operated hip past 90° (e.g. low chairs, low toilets, tying shoes).
  • Avoid crossing the operated leg over the midline of the body.
  • Avoid twisting the operated leg inward or outward.
  • Always follow the specific precautions given by your surgeon or therapist — they may differ from these general defaults.

Getting set up

  1. Prop your phone, tablet or laptop about 1.5–2 m away so your whole body fits in the frame.
  2. Sit or stand side-on to the camera for ROM training so the joint angle is clearly visible.
  3. Make sure the room is well lit and clothing contrasts with the background if possible.
  4. Allow camera access when your browser asks — video never leaves your device.
  5. Have a caregiver or therapist nearby the first few times you try a new exercise.
This tool is a training aid, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, and its angle readings are estimates from camera-based tracking, not clinical-grade goniometry. Always exercise within the limits set by your surgeon or therapist, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or instability.
